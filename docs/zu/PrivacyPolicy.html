Inqubomgomo Yobumfihlo

Isicelo:AiCasso

Usuku Lokusebenza:Ngo-December 17, 2025

Le Nqubomgomo Yobumfihlo ichaza ukuthi yiluphi ulwazi i-AiCasso oluqoqa futhi olucubungula, ukuthi lusetshenziswa kanjani, nezinketho onazo. I-AiCasso ngokuyinhloko uhlelo lokusebenza lokudala lwe-AI, futhi ifaka nezici zenhlalo ezingavunyelwe ezifana nokuthunyelwe kokuphakelayo komphakathi, ukuphawula, ukuthandwa, nokulandela.

Okubalulekile:Ikheli lakho le-imeyili lokungena ngemvume aliboniswa esidlangalaleni kuhlelo lokusebenza. I-AiCasso inikeza igama lomsebenzisi nephrofayili ekhiqizwa uhlelo iphrofayili yakho yomphakathi.

1) Imininingwane Esiyiqoqayo

Siqoqa imininingwane oyinikezayo, imininingwane eyenziwe ngokusebenzisa kwakho uhlelo lokusebenza, kanye nemininingwane edingekayo ukuze kusebenze izici zenhlalo ezingafanele.

  • Ama-akhawunti nezikhombisi:Uma ungena ngemvume ku-Google noma ku-Apple, sithola isazisi se-akhawunti kumhlinzeki futhi singathola nekheli lakho le-imeyili negama lakho (kuya ngokuthi umhlinzeki wabelane ngani). Sigcina igama lomsebenzisi elenziwe ngohlelo lomsebenzisi wephrofayili yakho yomphakathi.
  • Izwe/isifunda (esithathwe endaweni yendawo yedivayisi):Lapho ungena ngemvume, uhlelo lokusebenza luthumela ikhodi yezwe kanye nesifunda esithathwe kumasethingi endawo yedivayisi yakho.
  • Ukufakwa nokukhipha kwe-AI:Izikhombisi nemidiya oyihlinzekayo (isibonelo, izithombe ozilayishayo) kanye nokuqukethwe uhlelo lokusebenza olukhiqizayo (izithombe, umsindo, ividiyo, nemethadatha ehlobene nayo efana nemodi / izilungiselelo).
  • Okuqukethwe komphakathi (ngokuzithandela):Uma ukhetha ukubamba iqhaza ekuphakeleni komphakathi, sigcina futhi sibonise okuthunyelwe kwakho (izindaba ezenziwe yi-AI ezenziwe kuhlelo lokusebenza), kanye nemininingwane ehlobene elinganiselwe efana nesiphakamiso esiboniswa abanye (esihlanjululwe ukuboniswa komphakathi), izitembu zesikhathi, kanye nezibalo zokuzibandakanya.
  • Amazwana nezimpendulo (ngokuzithandela):Uma uphawula, sigcina umbhalo ophawulayo, okuthunyelwe / ukuphawula okuhlobene nakho, izitembu zesikhathi, nedatha yokuzibandakanya eyisisekelo (ukuthandwa, impendulo ibalwa).
  • Ukuxhumana nomphakathi (ngokuzithandela):Ukuthandwa, ukulandela, kanye nedatha ehlobene nobudlelwano/ukuzibandakanya esetshenziselwa ukunika amandla lezi zici.
  • Imibiko namabhulokhi (ngokuzithandela):Uma ubika okuqukethwe noma abasebenzisi, sigcina imininingwane yombiko oyithumeleyo (izizathu nemininingwane engenamkhawulo) nokuthi yini ebikwe. Uma uvimbela / uvula umsebenzisi, sigcina imininingwane edingekayo ukusebenzisa leyo nketho kuhlelo lokusebenza nokusekela ukuvimbela ukuhlukunyezwa.
  • Ukuthengwa nemivuzo:Isimo sokubhaliselwe nolwazi lokuthola / ukuthengiselana oludingekayo ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuthenga, kanye nesimo samaphoyinti / imivuzo (kufaka phakathi imivuzo etholwe ngezikhangiso lapho kufanele khona).
  • Izimemezelo ezicindezelayo (ngokuzithandela):Uma uvumela izaziso ze-push, sisebenzisa i-OneSignal ukuletha izaziso. I-OneSignal inikeza isazisi sedivayisi futhi singasihlobanisa nesazisi se-akhawunti yakho ye-AiCasso ukuletha izaziso ezithile ze-akhawunti.
  • Imibiko yokushayisana (ngokuzithandela):Uma ukhetha ukuthumela umbiko wokungasebenzi kahle, ungase ufake inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza, imodeli yedivayisi / i-OS, imininingwane yephutha, kanye ne-stack trace ukusisiza ukuxilonga izinkinga.
  • Ukukhangisa (ngokuzithandela):Uma uhlelo lokusebenza lubonisa izikhangiso, umhlinzeki wesikhangiso angaqoqa imininingwane njengengxenye yokulethwa nokulinganiswa kwesikhangiso.

2) Indlela Esisebenzisa Ngayo Ulwazi

  • Nikeza isevisi:Yakha okuqukethwe, uvumelanise imiklamo yakho namadivayisi lapho ungena ngemvume, futhi ubonise umtapo wakho wezincwadi.
  • Vumela izici zenhlalo (ngokuzithandela):Shicilela okuthunyelwe, bonisa iphrofayela yakho yomphakathi (igama lomsebenzisi / isithombe sephrofayela esenziwe uhlelo), vumela imibono, ukuthandwa, nokulandela.
  • Ukuphepha nobuqotho:Ukuphoqelela imigomo yethu yokusebenza kanye neziqondiso zomphakathi, ukucubungula imibiko, ukusebenzisa amabhulokhi, nokuthatha izinyathelo zokuhlukunyezwa (isibonelo, ukumiswa noma ukuvinjelwa).
  • Izinkokhelo nemivuzo:Qinisekisa ukuthenga / ukubhalisa nokuphatha amaphuzu / imiklomelo.
  • Izaziso:Thumela izaziso mayelana nomsebenzi ohambisanayo wohlelo lokusebenza (isibonelo, ukuxhumana nokuthunyelwe noma ukuphawula kwakho) uma ukhetha ukungena.
  • Ukusekela nokuthembeka:Phendula izicelo zokusekela futhi uphenye izingozi nezinkinga zokusebenza.

3) Lapho Ukwabelana Ngemininingwane

U-AiCasso wabelana ngolwazi ngezindlela ezilandelayo:

  • Ukwabelana nomphakathi (ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza):Uma uthumela noma uphawula, igama lakho lomsebenzisi nephrofayili yakho eyenziwe uhlelo, okuthunyelwe kwakho, nokuphawula kwakho kuyabonakala kwabanye abasebenzisi bohlelo lokusebenza. Isicelo esiboniswe kokuphakelwa kokuphakelayo sihlanjululwa ukuze siboniswe esidlangalaleni (isibonelo, ukususwa kwama-imeyili, izinombolo zocingo, nama-URL).
  • Abahlinzeki bezinsizakalo (abacubunguli):Sisebenzisa izinsizakalo zomuntu wesithathu ukusebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza (isibonelo, ukungena ngemvume, izaziso ze-push, ukusingathwa kwefu / isitoreji, izikhangiso, kanye nabahlinzeki bokukhiqizwa kwe-AI). Laba bahlinzeki bacubungula imininingwane egameni lethu ukuhlinzeka ngezinsizakalo zabo.
  • Umthetho nokuphepha:Singase sidalule ulwazi uma kudingeka ngokomthetho noma uma kudingeka ukuze sivikele abasebenzisi, sivimbele ukuhlukunyezwa, noma siphendule izicelo zomthetho.

4) Izinsizakalo Zomuntu Wesithathu

I-AiCasso ihlanganiswa nezigaba ezilandelayo zezinsizakalo zeqembu lesithathu:

  • Abahlinzeki bokungena ngemvume:Ngena ngemvume ku-Google futhi ungene ngemvume nge-Apple.
  • Izimemezelo ezicindezelayo:I-OneSignal.
  • Ukukhangisa:I-Google AdMob (uma izikhangiso ziboniswa ekwakhiweni kwakho).
  • Abahlinzeki bokucubungula i-AI:Sisebenzisa abahlinzeki be-AI bangaphandle ukukhiqiza nokuguqula okuqukethwe (ngokwesibonelo, ukukhiqizwa kwezithombe nezici ezihlobene nobuso). Laba bahlinzeki baqhuba iziphakamiso nemithombo yezindaba oyithumelayo ukuze ikhiqizwe.
  • Ingqalasizinda:I-backend yethu isebenzisa izinsizakalo zefu ukugcina idatha yezinhlelo zokusebenza nemidiya nokusebenza kokuphakelwa komphakathi.

Izinsizakalo zomuntu wesithathu zingaba nezinqubomgomo zazo zobumfihlo. Uma usebenzisa lezi zinsizakalo nge-AiCasso, imigomo nezinqubomgomo zazo zingasebenza futhi.

5) Ukugcinwa kwedatha

  • Izikhundla zomphakathi:Okuqukethwe okushicilela ku-feed kugcinwa uze ukuyisuse, ususe i-akhawunti yakho, noma siyisuse ngenxa yezizathu zokuphepha noma zenqubomgomo.
  • Amazwana nemisebenzi ehlobene nawo:Amazwana kanye nedatha yokuzibandakanya ehambisanayo agcinwa ukuze kusetshenziswe izici zomphakathi, ngaphandle kokuthi zisuswe ngenxa yezizathu zokuphepha noma zisuswe njengengxenye yokususwa kwe-akhawunti.
  • Ukudalwa nokulayishwa kwangasese:Imidiya nezizukulwane ezingashicilelwa esidlangalaleni zingasuswa ngokuzenzakalelayo njengengxenye yezinqubo zethu zokuhlanza (isibonelo, ngemuva kwewindi lokugcina elilinganiselwe noma lapho ama-URL we-upstream media engasatholakali).
  • Imibiko nemibhalo yokulinganisa:Imibiko namarekhodi ahlobene nokulinganisela angagcinwa lapho kudingeka khona ngokuphepha, ukuvimbela ukuhlukunyezwa nokucwaningwa kwamabhuku.
  • Ama-crash log:Imibiko yokushayisana igcinwa ukuze kuthuthukiswe ukulungisa amaphutha nokuthembeka.

6) Izinketho Zakho

  • Ukubamba iqhaza kwezenhlalakahle okungenamkhawulo:Ungasebenzisa i-AiCasso ngaphandle kokuthumela kokuphakelayo.
  • Ukubika nokuvimbela:Ungakwazi ukubika okuthunyelwe, ukuphawula, nabasebenzisi, futhi ungavimbela abanye abasebenzisi.
  • Izimemezelo ezicindezelayo:Ungakwazi ukuvumela noma ukukhubaza izaziso kusethingi yedivayisi yakho.
  • Ukususwa kwe-akhawunti:Ungaqala ukususwa kwe-akhawunti kuhlelo lokusebenza (Izilungiselelo → Ukususa i-akhawunti). Uma ungakwazi ukufinyelela kuhlelo lokusebenza, thintana nosizo futhi sizosiza.

7) Ukuphepha

Sisebenzisa izindlela zokuphepha ezifanele zokuphatha, ezobuchwepheshe nezingokwenyama ezenzelwe ukuvikela ukwaziswa.
