Imigomo Yokusebenza

Isicelo:AiCasso

Usuku Lokusebenza:Ngo-December 17, 2025

Le Migomo Yesevisi (Imigomo) ilawula ukusetshenziswa kwakho kwe-AiCasso (i-App) nezinsizakalo ezihlobene nayo (kuhlangene, i-Service).Inqubomgomo YobumfihloNgiyethemba .

Isaziso se-UGC:I-AiCasso ifaka izici zokuqukethwe ezizenzakalelayo zomsebenzisi (ukuthunyelwe, ukuphawula, ukuthandwa, ukulandela). Ukuze sisize ukugcina umphakathi uphephile, sinikeza imibiko ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza nokuvimbela umsebenzisi futhi singakwazi ukuphoqa le Migomo ngezenzo zokulinganisela.

1) Inkonzo

I-AiCasso ngokuyinhloko uhlelo lokusebenza lokudala lwe-AI. Ungakha okuqukethwe ukuze usebenzise wena. Ungakhetha futhi ukwabelana ngemikhiqizo eyenziwe yi-AI ekuphakeleni komphakathi futhi uxhumane nabanye abasebenzisi ngokusebenzisa imibono, ukuthandwa, nokulandela. Ukubamba iqhaza ekuphakeleni komphakathi kuyinto engakhethwa.

2) Ama-Akhawunti Nokubhalisa

  • Ukubhalisa okungakhethwayo:Ezinye izici zingasetshenziswa ngaphandle kokungena ngemvume. Ukuvumelanisa idatha phakathi kwamadivayisi nokusebenzisa izici zenhlalo, kungase kudingeke ungene ngemvume.
  • Ukubhalisa komuntu wesithathu:Sisekela i-Google Sign-In kanye ne-Apple Sign-in. Ukusetshenziswa kwakho kwalezo zinsizakalo nakho kungaphansi kwemigomo nezinqubomgomo zayo.
  • Iphrofayili yomphakathi:I-AiCasso inikeza igama lomsebenzisi nomfanekiso wephrofayili owenziwe uhlelo ongaboniswa kwabanye abasebenzisi ku-feed nakumazwana. Ikheli lakho le-imeyili lokungena ngemvume aliboniswa esidlangalaleni kuhlelo lokusebenza.

3) Okuqukethwe Komsebenzisi Nokuphakelayo Komphakathi

Okuqukethwe Komsebenzisi kuhlanganisa okuqukethwe okuhambisa noma okwenza kutholakale ngeSevisi, njengeziphakamiso, imithombo yezindaba elayishiwe, okukhiphayo okwenziwe, okuthunyelwe, ukuphawula nezimpendulo.

  • Amaposi okuphakela kuphela nge-AI:Ukuphakelwa komphakathi kungenxa yokwabelana ngemisebenzi eyenziwe yi-AI eyenziwe kuhlelo lokusebenza. Ukulayisha imithombo yezindaba ngokuzithandela ngqo ekuphakeleni komphakathi akuvunyelwe.
  • Ukubonakala:Uma uthumela noma uphawula, okuqukethwe kwakho nesithombe sakho somsebenzisi / sephrofayili esenziwe uhlelo lokusebenza kungabonakala kwabanye abasebenzisi bohlelo lokusebenza.
  • Umthwalo wakho wemfanelo:Unesibopho sokuqukethwe kwakho komsebenzisi nokuxhumana kwakho nabanye.
  • Amalungelo okufaka:Kumelwe ube namalungelo nezimvume ezidingekayo ukuze usebenzise noma yimuphi umbhalo, izithombe, umsindo noma ividiyo oyilayishayo noma oyihambisayo (kufaka phakathi izimvume ezivela kubantu ababoniswe ezithombeni, uma kudingeka).

4) Iziqondiso Zomphakathi (Okuqukethwe Okuphikisayo Akuvunyelwe)

Uyavuma ukuthi ngeke udale, uthumele, noma wabelane ngokuqukethwe komsebenzisi okungathandeki noma okulimazayo, kufaka phakathi okuqukethwe:

  • Kuyinto engekho emthethweni, kukhuthaza umsebenzi ongekho emthethweni, noma kwephula umthetho osebenzayo.
  • Lisongela, lihlukumeze, lihlukumeze, noma likhuthaze ubudlova noma ukuzilimaza.
  • Iqukethe inkulumo enenzondo noma ukubandlulula okubhekiswe emaqenjini avikelwe.
  • Iqukethe okuqukethwe ngokocansi okubandakanya izingane ezingaphansi kweminyaka yobudala noma izinto zokuhlukunyezwa ngokocansi kwezingane (lokhu akuvunyelwe ngokuqinile).
  • Iqukethe izithombe ezingathandeki noma ukuhlukunyezwa ngokocansi.
  • Kuphula amalungelo empahla yengqondo (amalungelo obunikazi, uphawu lokuhweba) noma amanye amalungelo abantu besithathu.
  • Iqukethe idatha yomuntu siqu ngabanye ngaphandle kwemvume (isibonelo, i-doxxing).
  • Ingabe i-spam, ekhohlisayo, noma ehloselwe ukuphatha ukuzibandakanya (isibonelo, ukuphawula okuphindaphindiwe, ukukhwabanisa).
  • Uzenza omunye umuntu noma udukise abanye ngokuthi ungubani.

5) Ukubika, Ukuvimbela Nokulinganisela

  • Ukubika:Uhlelo lokusebenza lunikeza amathuluzi angaphakathi nohlelo lokusebenza ukubika okuthunyelwe, ukuphawula nabasebenzisi. Imibiko ingabuyekezwa futhi isetshenziselwe ukuthatha isinyathelo.
  • Ukuvimbela:Uhlelo lokusebenza lunikeza amandla okuvimba abanye abasebenzisi, okusiza ugweme ukuxhumana okungafuneki nokuqukethwe.
  • Ukuhlunga:Singase sisebenzise izindlela ezifanele zokuvikela nokuhlola ukuze sinciphise okuqukethwe okungafanele nokuhlukunyezwa.
  • Ukuphoqelelwa:Singasusa noma sinciphise ukufinyelela kokuqukethwe, sifihle okuqukethwe, sinciphise izici, simise ama-akhawunti, noma sivimbele ama-akhawunti uma sikholelwa ukuthi le Migomo iyaphulwa noma ukuvikela abasebenzisi kanye nesevisi.

6) Ilayisense Oyinikeza I-AiCasso

Ugcina ubunikazi bokuqukethwe kwakho komsebenzisi phakathi kwakho no-AiCasso. Kodwa-ke, ukuze usebenzise isevisi, unikeza i-AiCasso ilayisense lomhlaba wonke, elingagciniwe, elingenayo amalungelo obunikazi, lokusingatha, ukulondoloza, ukukhiqiza, ukucubungula, ukubonisa nokusabalalisa okuqukethwe kwakho komsebenzisi: (a) ukukunikeza isevisi, futhi (b) uma ukhetha ukuthumela kokuphakelayo noma ukuphawula, ukwenza lokho okuqukethwe kutholakale kwabanye abasebenzisi ngaphakathi kwensizakalo. Le layisense iphela lapho ususa okuqukethwe komsebenzisi noma i-akhawunti yakho, ngaphandle kokuthi okuqukethwe kwabelwe nabanye futhi kufanele kugcinwe ngenxa yezizathu zokuphepha, zomthetho noma zokusebenza.

7) Izinsizakalo Zomuntu Wesithathu

Izinsizakalo zingahlanganiswa nezinsizakalo zabantu besithathu (isibonelo, abahlinzeki bokungena ngemvume, izaziso eziphuthumayo, izikhangiso, nabahlinzeki bokusebenza kwe-AI).

8) Izikhalazo

  • Le nsizakalo inikezwa njengoba ikhona futhi njengoba ikhona .
  • Imikhiqizo ekhiqizwa yi-AI ingahle ingalindelekile, ingaqondile, noma ifane nemikhiqizo ekhiqizwa abanye abasebenzisi.
  • Asiqinisekisi ukusebenza okungaphazanyiswa noma okungenawo amaphutha.

9) Ukulinganiselwa Komthwalo Wemfanelo

Ngokwezinga eliphakeme elivunyelwe ngumthetho, i-AiCasso ngeke ibe nesibopho semonakalo ongakaqondile, ongenzeki, okhethekile, owalandela noma owesabekayo ovela noma ohlobene nokusetshenziswa kwakho kweNsizakalo.

10) Izinguquko Kule Migomo

Singabuyekeza le Migomo ngezikhathi ezithile. Uma senza izinguquko ezibalulekile, sizobuyekeza usuku olusebenzayo futhi singanikeza isaziso esingeziwe ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza. Ukusetshenziswa kwakho okuqhubekayo kwensizakalo ngemuva kokuthi izinguquko zisebenze kusho ukuthi wamukela imigomo ebuyekeziwe.
